# Day 22 — AI Evaluation, Safety & Reliability

**Week 4: AI + DevOps Integration**  
**Estimated time:** 3–4 hours  
**Focus:** Golden datasets, accuracy, groundedness, latency, cost, prompt injection, privacy and human review.

## Learning objectives
- Define evaluation cases and metrics
- Measure groundedness and retrieval quality
- Mitigate prompt injection and data leakage
- Add human approval for risky actions
