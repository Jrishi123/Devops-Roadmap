# Day 18 — LLMs & Generative AI

**Week 3: AI Foundations**  
**Estimated time:** 3–4 hours  
**Focus:** Tokens, context windows, inference, temperature, system/user messages, hallucinations and model limits.

## Learning objectives
- Explain high-level LLM operation
- Understand tokens and context limits
- Choose generation settings appropriately
- Recognize hallucination and prompt injection risks

| Activity | Time |

1. Use an approved LLM playground or local mock
2. Compare concise vs detailed prompts
3. Test temperature and output constraints
4. Record failure cases and mitigations
